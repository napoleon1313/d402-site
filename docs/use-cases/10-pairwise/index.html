use case / 10-pairwise

10 pairwise

An answer-quality API sells scores on GET /v1/score. What it wants in return is the data such a scorer is built from: pairwise preferences, "which of these two answers to the prompt is better". The pool holds 120 pairwise items (SPEC section 8), each a prompt with two candidate answers where one is clearly better (a wrong sum, the wrong capital, a refusal, a hedge). The items come from a seeded generator in src/data.ts; which side holds the better answer is random and is the hidden truth. 30 items are golden.

The seller is created with pairwise: {} and no classify labels, so the offer lists taskTypes: ["pairwise"] and every bundle has type: "pairwise", labels: ["a", "b"] and items { id, prompt, a, b }. Three agents (claude, gpt, gemini) use createLookupSolver over the truth map with 90% accuracy (seeded), take turns earning one 15-item bundle per round until the seller has nothing new for them, then each spends one call on /v1/score.

Grading and resolution are the classify rules unchanged: goldens decide the voucher, unknown answers become weighted votes, and an item resolves when its top answer has weight 2.0 from at least 2 keys. With three voters that means all three agree. The seller exports the resolved items with d402.tasks.exportResolved() (pairwise rows carry type, prompt, a, b), writes them as { prompt, chosen, rejected } lines to out/preferences.jsonl, and scores them against the hidden truth.

sh
pnpm --filter d402-uc-pairwise run start

What you will see (abridged):

1. seller up at http://127.0.0.1:38589: 120 pairwise items (30 golden, 90 unknown), taskTypes ["pairwise"], tier "m": 15 items -> 20 calls to /v1/score
2. three agents pick the better answer until the seller has nothing new for them:
   claude (p=0.9): 10 bundles, 9 passed, 1 failed quality, seller's accuracy estimate 0.871, GET /v1/score -> 200
   gpt    (p=0.9): 10 bundles, 8 passed, 2 failed quality, seller's accuracy estimate 0.839, GET /v1/score -> 200
   gemini (p=0.9): 10 bundles, 9 passed, 1 failed quality, seller's accuracy estimate 0.871, GET /v1/score -> 200
3. seller exports resolved preferences with d402.tasks.exportResolved():
   31 of 90 pairs resolved (59 still open: the agents disagreed or a bundle failed), 30 bundles in total
   accuracy vs hidden truth: 31/31 = 100.0%

RESULT: 31/90 preferences resolved at 100.0% accuracy from 30 pairwise bundles; 3/3 agents paid for /v1/score with labels; export at .../out/preferences.jsonl

Coverage is the limit here, not accuracy. Of the 90 unknown pairs, 54 got a vote from all three agents and 31 of those were unanimous; the other 23 were split, and 36 pairs got only two votes because one agent failed the bundle that held them (a failed bundle's answers are discarded). Those 59 stay open until a fourth agent labels them. The run is deterministic: same seed, same numbers.
